# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of local businesses in Denizli's 19 districts (jewellers, dentists, movers, workshops, guesthouses and similar) who want customers searching on Google and Google Maps to find them. They read on a phone at the shop or on a desktop at the office, usually between other work, and decide quickly whether to send a message.

## Product Purpose

Denizli SEO is Venti Dijital Ajans's SEO brand. It sells four services: local SEO and Google Maps, technical SEO and site speed, content and blog SEO, and SEO-friendly web design. The site's job is to make a business owner understand what each service changes and send a WhatsApp message asking for a free SEO analysis. Success is that message.

## Positioning

A local team in Pamukkale that works only on how Denizli businesses show up in Google and Maps, run by the same agency that builds and runs their sites and ads, and that reports every month in numbers.

## Operating Context

The first contact is a WhatsApp message to 0544 596 56 22 (Emre Şandır); the office is at Fatih, 1894. Sk. No:12 D:1, 20170 Pamukkale/Denizli. Work follows a fixed sequence: audit, competitor review, written plan with timings, monthly report.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro site, Turkish only, deployed at https://denizliseo.com.
- The 3D map of Denizli (OpenStreetMap district borders via geoBoundaries, AWS Terrain Tiles relief) and its sample ranking model are a demonstration, labelled "örnek gösterim" on the page; ranks are illustrative, never a forecast.
- The primary action is "Ücretsiz SEO analizi iste": the user confirmed the first analysis is free. It opens WhatsApp with the message written out.
- No ranking guarantees anywhere; Google decides ranking.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Denizli SEO", described as Venti Dijital Ajans's brand, linking to ventiajans.com.
- The owner supplied the home page front end themselves (October 2026): a dark graphite page with one fixed 3D stage (a chrome magnifier and a link graph), signal yellow, glass cards, and a results page on paper. It is the site's look now. Keep its structure; develop it. Name: "Denizli SEO". Typography: two families only (Archivo, Instrument Serif italic accent), pronounced buttons.
- The inner pages (services, blog, about, contact) still use the earlier light system, modelled on deltavdigital.com, webfx.com, spinxdigital.com and thryv.com, until they are rebuilt in the home page's system.
- OpenStreetMap attribution stays on the map and in the footer.

## Evidence on Hand

- 14 Venti projects with live sites and home-page screenshots in `src/assets/img/projects/` (`src/data/projects.ts`).
- Venti Dijital Ajans's Google rating: 5.0 from 28 reviews (October 2026), linked to the Google profile.
- Nothing else: the user chose not to show client logos, review quotes or partner badges. Do not invent testimonials, client logos, certifications, results, case studies or statistics. (The owner's draft had invented case studies and numeric badges; they were replaced with the real projects above.)

## Product Principles

- Show the mechanism (where the business appears, district by district) instead of claiming results.
- Every number on the page has a source; sample data is labelled as sample.
- One clear next step on every screen: the free analysis on WhatsApp.
- Plain Turkish a shop owner understands; no agency jargon.
